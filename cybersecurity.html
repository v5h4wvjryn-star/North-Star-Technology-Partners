What We Do

Layered protection, managed for you

No single product stops every attack. We combine the right controls, keep them up to date and watch for trouble.

Security Assessment

A clear review of your current risks (accounts, devices, network, backups and policies) with a prioritized plan to fix them.

Endpoint Detection & Response

Advanced protection on every computer and server that detects and stops threats traditional antivirus misses.

Email Security

Filtering for phishing, malware and impersonation before it ever reaches your inbox.

Identity & Access

Multi-factor authentication, strong password practices and right-sized permissions, so a stolen password isn’t a stolen business.

Backup & Ransomware Recovery

Protected, tested backups so you can recover from ransomware or a disaster without paying anyone.

Security Awareness Training

Short, practical training and simulated phishing that turn your team into a line of defense.

Firewall & Network Security

Properly configured firewalls, segmented networks and secure remote access.

Policies & Incident Response

Written security policies and a plan for what to do, and who to call, if something goes wrong.
