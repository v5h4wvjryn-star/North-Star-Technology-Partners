In the Real World

What this looks like in practice

Lawn & Tree Service
The Problem
Job notes lived in a group text thread. Crews couldn’t find prior job history, and billing disputes were constant because nothing was documented at the job site.
The Fix
A simple mobile-first job documentation app with photos, digital sign-off and an automatic PDF summary sent to the customer after every visit.
The Result
Billing disputes dropped to near zero, and crew handoffs stopped relying on memory.
HVAC Company
The Problem
Service reminders were handled manually by the office admin. About half went out and the rest were forgotten, so seasonal revenue was unpredictable.
The Fix
An automated follow-up workflow triggered by job close date, sending text and email reminders at 6 and 11 months with a direct booking link.
The Result
Seasonal maintenance bookings increased significantly, and the admin spends no time on follow-up.
Insurance Agency
The Problem
Policy renewals were tracked in a spreadsheet. Agents checked it weekly and emailed customers one by one, and things fell through the cracks.
The Fix
A lightweight renewal dashboard with automated outreach at 90, 60 and 30 days, personalized by policy type, plus a live view of what’s due each week.
The Result
No more missed renewals. Agents spend their time closing instead of chasing calendars.

These are illustrative examples based on common problems we solve.
