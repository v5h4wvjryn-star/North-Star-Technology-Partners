FAQ

Common questions

How much does managed IT cost?

Pricing depends on the number of users and devices, your plan and any compliance needs. We don’t publish one-size-fits-all pricing. After a free assessment you get a clear per-user quote with no surprises.

What does after-hours support cover?

Our extended support hours run 7 AM to 8 PM, Monday through Friday. Weekday evenings from 8 PM to 7 AM and all weekend count as after hours, when we cover urgent and critical issues according to your plan: available on request with Essentials, included for critical outages with Professional and included for urgent issues with Complete.

What size businesses do you work with?

We focus on small businesses and growing mid-sized companies. As you grow, we add specialist partner resources so your support scales with you.

We already have an IT person. Can you still help?

Yes. We can work alongside an in-house IT person in a co-managed arrangement, handling monitoring, security, after-hours coverage or projects so they can focus on what they do best.

We’re already a client. How do we get help?

Email support@nstp.tech to open a request. During extended hours (7 AM – 8 PM, Monday – Friday) it goes straight to our help desk, and after hours, urgent issues are handled according to your plan.

Do you come on-site?

Yes, across the Dallas–Fort Worth area. Most issues are solved remotely and faster, but when someone needs to be there in person, we are.

What happens if you’re unavailable?

We maintain relationships with vetted partner technicians and document your environment thoroughly, so support continues even when the primary engineer is out.
